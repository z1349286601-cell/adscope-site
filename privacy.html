This policy explains how __COMPANY_NAME__ ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our advertising research software and this website. It applies to our business customers and to visitors to this website.

In short: our product works with advertising data that platforms publish publicly. We do not build profiles of individual consumers, we do not run advertising or tracking technologies on this website, and we do not sell data to anyone.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Public advertising data

Our software collects advertising information that platforms are legally required to disclose publicly, and information returned by official platform interfaces that we are authorised to use. This information concerns advertisements and advertisers, not private individuals. It typically includes:

  • the advertisement identifier, publication date and last-seen date;
  • the advertiser's public page name and identifier;
  • the public creative material and any public text accompanying the advertisement;
  • the markets in which the advertisement was shown;
  • audience information in the aggregated, banded form published by the platform;
  • the public landing page address associated with the advertisement.

2.2 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect names, contact details or identifiers of individual consumers.
  • We do not create individual audience profiles, and we do not attempt to identify who saw an advertisement.
  • We do not use cookies, pixels, analytics scripts or advertising identifiers on this website.
  • We do not purchase or ingest personal data from data brokers.

2.3 Business contact information

If you contact us by email, we hold your name, email address and the content of your message in order to reply to you.

4. Sources

Public advertising data is obtained from platform advertising transparency archives, from official platform interfaces under approved access agreements, and from publicly accessible pages that do not require authentication. Where a platform provides an authorised interface, that interface is the primary source for the corresponding data.

5. How long we keep information

  • Public advertising data is retained for as long as the underlying platform makes the advertisement available, and is removed from our active dataset within 30 days of the advertisement no longer being published or of its record expiring at the source. In practice this means a maximum retention period of approximately one year following the last date on which an advertisement was shown.
  • Business contact information is retained for as long as the business relationship continues, and for up to 24 months after the last correspondence.

6. Sharing

We do not sell data. We share information only with service providers who host and operate our systems under written instructions, and where we are required to do so by law. Those providers act as processors and are bound to use the information only for the purposes we specify.
