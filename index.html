Public Advertising Research

Structured access to publicly available advertising data for e-commerce teams.

AdScope builds research software that organises publicly disclosed advertising information by product category and market, so that teams can study how products are presented to consumers in Europe.

What the product does

Advertising transparency archives publish a large volume of information, but they are designed for individual searches rather than for systematic study. Our software collects this public information on a scheduled basis and presents it in a form that a business team can review.

Category views

Public advertisements are grouped into product categories such as apparel, beauty and kitchenware, using a maintained keyword set.

Creative comparison

Teams can compare how different advertisers present similar products, using the creative material and copy that platforms publish.

Market coverage

Results are organised by market, with Germany and the United Kingdom as the initial coverage areas.

Where the data comes from

All information presented by our software is advertising data that platforms are legally required to make publicly available under transparency regulation, together with data returned by official platform interfaces.

We do not collect personal data about advertising audiences. Our software does not build individual profiles, does not use tracking pixels, and does not attempt to identify any person who has seen an advertisement. Audience information is used only in the aggregated form that platforms themselves publish.

We use official interfaces where they exist. Where a platform provides an authorised programming interface for public advertising data, we use that interface under the terms of an approved access agreement. Where no such interface exists, we study only content that is publicly accessible without authentication.

Who uses it

Primary users
Media buyers and product teams at cross-border e-commerce companies.
Typical task
Reviewing newly published advertisements in a category, and deciding which creative approaches are worth studying further.
Coverage today
Apparel, beauty and kitchenware categories; Germany and the United Kingdom.
What it is not
The software does not provide audience targeting, does not predict advertising performance, and does not report spend or conversion figures.
