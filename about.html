What AdScope does

AdScope is an independent project that studies advertising data which platforms publish publicly under transparency regulation. The project collects that data through official platform interfaces, organises it by product category and by market, and presents it in a form that a business team can review.

The coverage is Europe, with Germany and the United Kingdom as the starting markets, and apparel, beauty and kitchenware as the starting categories.

Why it exists

Transparency regulation has made a large amount of advertising information public, but the public archives are built for one search at a time. Answering a question like "how are similar products being presented to European consumers this month" means running many searches, keeping the results, and recognising the same advertiser across them. AdScope is an attempt to make that work repeatable.

How the data is handled

  • Where a platform provides an authorised interface for public advertising data, that interface is used under an approved access agreement.
  • Only information that platforms themselves publish is processed; no attempt is made to collect data about individual consumers.
  • No audience profiles are built, no tracking technologies are used, and raw datasets are not resold or redistributed.

The full detail is set out in the Privacy Policy.
